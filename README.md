# Project Details
This project focuses on building a full-funnel digital marketing strategy for Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters to strengthen D2C growth, subscriptions, brand storytelling, and customer engagement. The strategy combines SEO, Google Ads, social media, email marketing, content marketing, and emotional brand positioning to improve organic visibility, customer retention, and online revenue growth. 

# The project includes:

. Brand positioning and competitor analysis
. Customer persona and audience research
. Emotional campaign strategy development
. SEO and keyword cluster planning
. Content marketing and blogging strategy
. Pillar-cluster SEO architecture
. Google Ads and PPC campaign planning
. YouTube and Meta Ads strategy
. Social media content funnel and calendar planning
. Influencer and UGC campaign strategy
. Email marketing automation and segmentation
. Conversion-focused D2C funnel optimization
. Performance tracking and KPI framework
. Subscription growth and retention strategy
. Integrated multi-channel digital marketing planning
. The project highlights how storytelling, educational content, SEO, paid advertising, and community-driven marketing can help premium D2C brands improve engagement, subscriptions, and long-term customer loyalty.
